# Proposal: remote-mutation-names-target-effect-reversal

On a real target device the agent changed a system setting to unblock a queued job (it made the device report a charger), with the reversal written into its announced plan beforehand. Afterwards every changed setting was read back.

## Result

2026-11-11. One write on a device under the owner's explicit request to finish a transfer. The plan named the target, the effect and the reversal before running; the reversal ran, and the read-back found every setting restored (power source, the session's wake lock and the job state), with nothing left to repair. The read-back was asked for by the owner, not done by the agent unprompted, so the experiment confirms that a reversal written down is executed, and does not confirm that its verification follows without a prompt.
