A component retrained on every build, here a compression dictionary, makes successive artefacts share almost nothing at the page level, so delta updates save nothing. Pin it across builds and retrain only when the content changes shape; in the measured case pinning cost a small percentage of payload size.

## Evidence

One repository, measured on two real consecutive builds: a fixed-offset differ saved nothing; a rolling-checksum differ ranged from most of an artefact to almost nothing across artefacts of the same pair. The owner decided to pin, retraining only when the content shifts.
