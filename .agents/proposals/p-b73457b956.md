A counter-boundary to the bootstrap's commit rule (a commit whose subject needs an "and" is two commits): splitting by dependency does not license one-line commits. Group by context, files touched and kind of change, and ask whether pieces can go together before asking whether one could go apart.

## Evidence

One repository: the human answered a three-option question about a decision with a correction about commit granularity instead, saying a series cannot be split into commits of one line each and should be grouped by context, files and type of change; the agent recorded it in local memory and followed it afterwards.
