Before reading a measured number, write down the condition that would invalidate it (activity during the window, the target still reachable inside the loop, a sample independent of the quantity measured, a search pattern that cannot match the observer's own commands) and print the reason every discarded window was discarded. A plausible wrong number is otherwise indistinguishable from a right one. Costs minutes per measurement. Overlaps principle 6 and the completion-check form of `a-check-must-be-seen-to-fail`.

## Evidence

One repository. Before the period, five false numbers in one read-only device session, all plausible. In the period: a size sample drawn by row id, where the id correlates with frequency, biased the estimate in both directions (one tail overstated about twofold), and the session log names the missing criterion as the standing rule it broke; a split-half control was invalid because both halves were ordered by the same correlate; a word-count rule reported most items as empty and was caught because a criterion had been written first; three measurements were wrong before right in one session; a process monitor matching on the full command line matched the agent's own waiting shells and reported a failed pipeline as running for twenty minutes; a log search matched the agent's own commands; a transfer progress bar counted bytes handed to the transport and read complete with about three quarters written.

Literature: preregistration of analyses (Nosek et al., 2018); ASSUMPTION, not checked against the source.
