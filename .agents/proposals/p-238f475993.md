From a second repository, and from a constraint to a cost estimate: a cost recorded as fact frames every decision taken against it until it is measured, and the first correction is itself a measurement that needs checking.

## Evidence

A pipeline rebuild recorded as taking about an hour framed a whole day of include-it-now-or-pay-again choices; measured, it took about a quarter of that, and the first correction was off by an order of magnitude the other way because it timed only the fast half. A dependency's removal was priced by its size before the resolved dependency graph showed it was already present transitively. A size figure extrapolated from an unrepresentative sample was nearly double the measured one, and a code comment on the same option was wrong by more than an order of magnitude. A session answered that nothing was implementable without hardware, a rebuild or a decision, treating hours of compute as a blocker; the human pushed back and the sweep found every input available.
