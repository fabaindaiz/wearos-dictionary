# Proposal: explain-a-term-before-asking-about-it

A question to the owner that turns on a technical term gets the term explained by one concrete example inside the question, because the owner picks an option either way and the confusion surfaces only later, as a question about a choice already recorded.

## Evidence

One carrier, one owner, three occurrences in about forty question answers over a month. The owner accepted the recommended build type for a target device, whose name suggests a benchmark runs there, and twice afterwards asked why a benchmark was on the device; the build type's name, not its role, was what they had read. In a decision about a stored identifier column, the owner's free-text answer asked what the identifier was before choosing. In a third, the free-text answer said they did not understand what the question was about while still picking an option. Each was caught only because the owner wrote free text or asked later. Literature: the candidate's own; no new source consulted.
